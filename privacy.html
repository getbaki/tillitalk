🌙 TilliTalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. About TilliTalk

TilliTalk is a children's educational language-learning application developed and operated by Tales by Moonlight.

Website: talesbymoonlight.nl

Privacy contact: hello@talesbymoonlight.nl

2. Information We Process

Children can use TilliTalk's learning activities without creating a personal account or providing their name, email address, or home address.

The application and its service providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, connection details, and request timestamps, to deliver lessons and maintain the service.

Certain preferences and learning resources may be stored locally in the user's browser or device.

3. Children's Privacy

Tales by Moonlight is committed to protecting children's privacy and minimizing the personal information needed to use TilliTalk.

Children do not need to register or submit personal contact details to participate in learning activities. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us about privacy concerns.

4. Educational Pictures and Videos

TilliTalk displays educational pictures, videos, questions, and answers to support language development.

Authorized administrators manage this content through a separate administration system. Published educational content is delivered through technical service providers, including Supabase.

5. Voice Features

TilliTalk uses text-to-speech functionality to read learning questions, words, and answers aloud.

Children are not required to record their voices or submit voice recordings to Tales by Moonlight. Speech playback may use services provided by the user's device or browser.

6. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Display educational lessons and learning materials.
  • Provide voice playback and learning preferences.
  • Maintain the application's functionality and security.
  • Manage educational content through authorized administrators.
  • Respond to support and privacy inquiries.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not use children's personal information for behavioral advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

TilliTalk uses Supabase for database, storage, authentication, and related application services. Hosting and infrastructure providers may also process limited technical information needed to operate the application.

Tales by Moonlight does not sell children's personal information.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with TilliTalk, including administrator authentication, access restrictions, and secure connections to backend services.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application preferences and resources may remain on a device until they are cleared or removed. Educational content is retained while needed for the learning service or until removed by an administrator.

Administrator information and technical records are retained as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, and legal obligations.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable, please email hello@talesbymoonlight.nl .

10. Privacy Rights

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal information is processed on an appropriate legal basis, such as legitimate interests, legal obligations, or consent where required.

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to certain processing of their personal information. They may also contact a relevant data protection authority.

11. Changes to This Policy

Tales by Moonlight may update this policy to reflect changes to TilliTalk, its services, or legal requirements. The updated policy will be published here with a revised date.
